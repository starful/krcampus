## Where This Program Sits: Songdo, Not Old Incheon

Incheon National University (INU) is a national university, and its Korean Language Institute (한국어교육원) operates on the Songdo campus in Yeonsu-gu — not downtown Incheon or near Incheon Station. Songdo is a purpose-built international business district about 40 minutes from Seoul by subway (Incheon Line 1 to AREX or Line 1 transfer), and roughly 15 minutes from Incheon International Airport by airport bus. This matters for students choosing between a 'Seoul school' and a 'quieter, cheaper alternative': INU-KLI gives you Seoul access without Seoul rent, plus proximity to Songdo Central Park, large international residential blocks, and a lower cost of living than Hongdae, Sinchon, or Gangnam-adjacent institutes.

## Program Structure and Class Levels

The Regular Program runs six proficiency levels (1 through 6), each spanning one 10-week quarter, with four intakes per year in March, June, September, and December. Class size is capped near 15 students to keep speaking practice active. Placement is done via a written and oral test on orientation day for anyone who has prior Korean study; complete beginners start at Level 1 automatically.

| Level | Focus | Typical TOPIK Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Survival Korean, basic grammar, Hangul mastery | Pre-TOPIK / TOPIK I Level 1 |
| 3-4 | Intermediate grammar, everyday topics, presentations | TOPIK I Level 2 / TOPIK II Level 3 |
| 5-6 | Advanced reading, debate, academic writing | TOPIK II Level 4-5 |

## Tuition and Mandatory Fees

Costs are quoted in Korean won and billed per quarter. Compared with many Seoul-based university language institutes, INU-KLI tends to run slightly below the Seoul average, though the gap is modest.

| Item | Amount (KRW) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Application/registration fee | 80,000 | One-time, non-refundable |
| Quarterly tuition | 1,600,000 | Per 10-week term |
| Textbook fee | 70,000 | Per level, paid at start of term |
| Health insurance (if required) | ~150,000/quarter | Mandatory for D-4 visa holders |
| Estimated total per quarter | ~1,750,000-1,900,000 | Tuition + textbooks + insurance |

Students should budget separately for Songdo housing, which runs cheaper than central Seoul: shared officetels near the campus average 400,000-550,000 KRW per month, versus 600,000+ in Hongdae or Sinchon.

## Housing Reality: Limited Dormitory, Strong Off-Campus Market

Unlike some private universities with large dedicated international dorms, INU's on-campus housing for language students is limited and often prioritized for degree-track exchange students. Most KLI students end up in nearby officetels or goshiwons in Songdo's residential blocks (Convensia-daero and the area around Central Park), which are newer and more apartment-like than typical Seoul goshiwon stock because Songdo was built recently as a planned city. Budget 400,000-600,000 KRW/month for a studio, plus a deposit (bojeungeum) that can range from 1,000,000 to 5,000,000 KRW depending on the landlord and lease length. Real estate agents (부동산) near Incheon National University Station handle short-term contracts aimed at language students.

## D-4 Visa Considerations Specific to This School

As with any accredited Korean university language program, six-month or longer enrollment in the Regular Program qualifies students for a D-4-1 (Korean language training) visa. Required documents typically include the certificate of admission from INU-KLI, proof of financial capacity (bank statement showing sufficient funds, generally around 10,000 USD equivalent or the KRW equivalent, though this varies by applicant's home country and immigration office discretion), a completed visa application, and passport photos. Because INU is a national university, some students report slightly smoother visa-office review compared with lesser-known private academies, though outcomes still depend on the applicant's home-country immigration office. Renewal each quarter requires proof of continued enrollment and, from Level 3 onward, attendance records — INU-KLI enforces an attendance floor (commonly 80%) below which visa renewal recommendation can be withheld.

## TOPIK Preparation and Testing Access

INU-KLI integrates TOPIK-style mock exams into Levels 3 through 6, with dedicated review sessions before the official test dates (typically held four times a year nationwide). Because Incheon hosts its own TOPIK test center, students don't need to travel into Seoul to sit the exam, which reduces one source of test-day stress. Institute staff also help with TOPIK registration logistics, including the online application window that opens roughly six weeks before each test date.

## Campus Life and the Songdo Trade-Off

Songdo is quieter and more car/bus-oriented than dense Seoul neighborhoods, which is a genuine trade-off: fewer late-night convenience options and nightlife, but cleaner streets, wide bike paths, and a large expat and international-student population thanks to nearby Yonsei International Campus and other institutions. The KLI runs a buddy program pairing new international students with Korean undergraduates, plus cultural field trips (kimchi-making, palace visits, DMZ tours) roughly once per quarter. For students who want to combine study with easy airport access — useful for those planning weekend trips home or regional travel — Songdo's proximity to Incheon International Airport is a practical advantage few Seoul-based institutes can match.

## Application Timeline and Required Documents

Applications for each quarter generally close about two months before the term start (e.g., early January for the March intake). Required documents include a completed application form, a copy of the passport, a bank statement or financial sponsor documents, a copy of the highest diploma or enrollment certificate, and passport-sized photos. Documents from certain countries flagged by Korean immigration for stricter visa review may require additional apostille or consular authentication — applicants from these regions should start the document-gathering process at least one month earlier than the standard deadline to avoid missing the intake.

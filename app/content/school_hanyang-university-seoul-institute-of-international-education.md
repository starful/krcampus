## Why Wangsimni Matters More Than the Brochure Says

Hanyang University's Institute of International Education sits inside the university's main Seoul campus in Seongdong-gu, not in a satellite building rented out for language classes. That distinction shapes daily life here: classrooms, the library, gyms, and cafeterias are shared with Korean undergraduates, and the subway hub at Wangsimni Station (Line 2, Bundang Line, and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line) puts Gangnam, Hongdae, and Dongdaemun within a 20-30 minute ride. For learners weighing several Seoul institutes, the practical question isn't just tuition — it's whether you want a language program embedded in a research university's daily rhythm or a standalone academy. Hanyang leans firmly toward the former.

## Term Calendar and 2026 Intake Windows

The program runs four ten-week terms per year, aligning with the standard KLI (Korean Language Institute) cycle used across most Seoul universities. New students should apply roughly 2-3 months before their target term to clear visa processing.

| Term | Approx. Start | Application Deadline (int'l) |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | Early March | Late December |
| Summer | Early June | Late March |
| Fall | Early September | Late June |
| Winter | Early December | Late September |

Each term totals roughly 200 class hours (4 hours/day, 5 days/week), which satisfies the attendance threshold Korean immigration expects for D-4-1 visa holders.

## Tuition, Fees, and What's Not Included

Costs are billed per term rather than per year, which matters for budgeting since most students commit to at least 2-4 terms to reach an intermediate level.

| Item | Amount (KRW) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Application/registration fee | 80,000 | One-time, non-refundable |
| Tuition per term | 1,650,000 | Due before term start |
| Textbook fee | 80,000 | Institute-published series |
| Dormitory (per term, if selected) | 1,000,000-1,300,000 | Shared room, limited slots |
| Health insurance | ~150,000/term | Required for D-4 visa holders |

Budget separately for the Alien Registration Card (30,000 KRW), notarized document fees in your home country, and initial deposit for private housing if you don't secure a dorm bed — Seongdong-gu studio deposits typically start around 3-5 million KRW plus monthly rent of 500,000-700,000 KRW.

## Level Structure and How Placement Actually Works

The program runs six proficiency levels, from absolute beginner through advanced academic Korean. New applicants without prior study start at Level 1; returning or transfer students sit a placement test covering grammar, reading, and a short interview.

| Level | Focus | Typical TOPIK Alignment |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Survival grammar, Hangeul mastery, daily conversation | Pre-TOPIK I |
| 3-4 | Intermediate grammar, presentations, short essays | TOPIK II (Levels 3-4) |
| 5-6 | Academic writing, debate, media Korean | TOPIK II (Levels 5-6) |

Levels 5-6 classes deliberately mirror the reading and writing demands of Hanyang's own undergraduate courses, since a meaningful share of students use the institute as a direct bridge into the university's Korean-taught degree programs.

## From Language Track to Hanyang Degree Admission

This is where the institute distinguishes itself from academies with no university attached. Students who complete Level 4 or higher and hold a qualifying TOPIK score can apply to Hanyang's undergraduate or graduate programs through the international admissions track without needing a separate outside language certificate — the institute's own completion certificate and internal assessment carry weight in that internal pipeline. If your goal is eventual enrollment at Hanyang specifically, finishing the language program here rather than elsewhere removes a layer of document translation and verification.

## Housing Reality Near Wangsimni

On-campus dormitory space is limited and allocated by application order, so students planning to arrive for the March or September terms (the most competitive intakes) should apply for housing the moment enrollment is confirmed. Those who don't secure a dorm room typically look at officetels in Haengdang-dong or Sageun-dong, both within walking distance of the main gate. Goshiwon rooms nearby run cheaper (350,000-450,000 KRW/month) but suit short stays better than a full academic year.

## Visa Paperwork Specific to This Institute

Beyond the standard D-4-1 checklist (passport, admission letter, financial proof of roughly 10,000 USD or equivalent, apostilled diploma), Hanyang's institute requires its own financial guarantee form signed by the sponsor and a bank statement no older than one month at the time of submission. Processing through the Seoul Immigration office serving Seongdong-gu typically takes 2-4 weeks once the institute issues the certificate of admission, so submitting documents at the deadline rather than after it is not optional if you want to make the term start date.

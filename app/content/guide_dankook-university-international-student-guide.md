# Dankook University Guide: Tuition, Programs & Housing 2026

Dankook University (단국대학교, DKU) is a large private research university with two distinct campuses — Jukjeon in Yongin (near Seoul) and Cheonan in South Chungcheong Province. Founded in 1947, it's known for dentistry, medicine, engineering, and film/media programs, and hosts one of the larger international student bodies among mid-tier Korean private universities. If you're comparing tuition, dorm costs, or figuring out which campus fits your major, here's the breakdown.

## Jukjeon vs. Cheonan: Which Campus Actually Matters for You

Most undergraduate international students land at the **Jukjeon campus** (Yongin, Gyeonggi-do), about 40 minutes from central Seoul by bus/subway combination — this is often marketed loosely as "Seoul campus" even though it's technically in Yongin. Jukjeon hosts business, engineering, liberal arts, and the well-regarded College of Theatre, Film & Dance.

The **Cheonan campus**, roughly 1 hour south by KTX or intercity bus, houses Dentistry, Medicine, Pharmacy, and several applied science and sports-related departments. If your major is health sciences, you'll likely be on Cheonan regardless of preference. Housing, dining, and shuttle schedules differ between the two, so confirm your department's campus assignment before signing a dorm contract — switching later is a hassle since dorms are campus-specific.

## Tuition by Degree Level and College

Tuition at Dankook is charged per semester and varies significantly by college (humanities/business are cheapest; engineering, arts, and medicine/dentistry are most expensive). Figures below are approximate 2026 per-semester costs for international students in KRW and USD (at ~1,380 KRW/USD).

| Program Level | College Type | Per-Semester Tuition (KRW) | Approx. USD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Undergraduate | Humanities/Business/Social Sciences | ₩3,600,000–4,100,000 | $2,600–3,000 |
| Undergraduate | Engineering/Natural Sciences | ₩4,500,000–5,200,000 | $3,300–3,800 |
| Undergraduate | Arts/Film/Design | ₩5,000,000–5,800,000 | $3,600–4,200 |
| Undergraduate | Dentistry/Medicine (Cheonan) | ₩8,000,000–9,500,000 | $5,800–6,900 |
| Graduate (Master's) | General Graduate School | ₩4,200,000–5,500,000 | $3,000–4,000 |
| Graduate (PhD) | General Graduate School | ₩4,800,000–6,200,000 | $3,500–4,500 |

Add a one-time admission fee (~₩200,000–300,000) and mandatory student health insurance (~₩50,000/semester) on top. Graduate tuition runs higher in lab-based disciplines due to equipment fees. Tuition typically rises 2–3% annually, so budget on the higher end of each range if applying for 2027 intake.

## English-Taught vs. Korean-Taught Tracks

Dankook offers a mixed model — not every major has a full English track, so this matters more here than at SKY-tier schools with dedicated international colleges.

- **Fully English-taught undergraduate programs**: limited but growing, concentrated in Business Administration, International Trade, and select Engineering tracks. These typically require IELTS 5.5+/TOEFL 80+/TOEIC 700+ in place of TOPIK.
- **Korean-taught undergraduate majors** (the majority): require **TOPIK Level 3** minimum for direct admission, though TOPIK 4+ is strongly preferred for competitive majors like Dentistry-adjacent sciences or Film.
- **Graduate school**: many master's and PhD programs allow coursework in English even if officially "Korean-taught," especially in Engineering and Business departments with international faculty — but thesis defense and some seminars may still require working Korean.
- **Korean Language Program (KLI)**: Dankook runs its own language institute on both campuses, with 10-week terms, useful for building TOPIK scores before degree admission or as a direct track into year-one coursework.

If your Korean is below TOPIK 3, plan on one or two KLI terms first — this is standard, not a setback, and many students use it to scout the campus before committing to a major.

## Dormitory Costs, Eligibility, and Deadlines

Both campuses have dedicated international/mixed dorms, but capacity is tighter at Jukjeon given its proximity to Seoul — apply the moment the portal opens.

| Dorm Type | Campus | Monthly Cost (approx.) | Room Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| International Dorm A | Jukjeon | ₩450,000–550,000 | Double, shared bath |
| International Dorm B | Jukjeon | ₩550,000–650,000 | Double, private bath |
| Cheonan Global Dorm | Cheonan | ₩400,000–500,000 | Double/Triple, shared bath |
| Single Room (limited) | Both | ₩650,000–750,000 | Single, private bath |

Costs are usually billed per semester (4–4.5 months), not monthly, so multiply accordingly for your actual payment (~₩1.8–2.6 million per semester). Meal plans are separate and optional, averaging ₩800,000–1,000,000/semester if you opt in.

**Eligibility**: new international students get priority for the first year; continuing students must maintain a minimum GPA (often 2.5–2.75/4.5) to requalify, and dorms are generally not guaranteed beyond year one unless scholarship-linked.

**Deadlines**: dorm applications open roughly 2 months before each semester — early-to-mid June for Fall (September start) and early-to-mid December for Spring (March start). Dankook dorms fill fast for Jukjeon specifically because of Seoul-adjacent demand; apply within the first week of the window if possible.

## Scholarships International Students Actually Qualify For

Dankook's international scholarship structure is tuition-reduction based rather than cash stipends, stacked in tiers:

- **New Student Scholarship**: 30–50% tuition reduction for admitted international freshmen/transfers, based on prior academic records (GPA equivalent and TOPIK/English scores).
- **GKS (Global Korea Scholarship) host institution**: Dankook is an occasional host university for GKS graduate scholars, covering full tuition plus a monthly stipend (~₩1,000,000) — apply through the Korean Embassy/NIIED in your home country, not directly through Dankook, roughly 8–9 months before intended entry.
- **Continuing Student Scholarship**: 10–30% renewal-based reduction tied to maintaining GPA above ~3.0/4.5 each semester — this is where many students lose aid, so treat semester 1 GPA seriously.
- **Dankook International Scholarship (DIS)**: merit-based, awarded at admission for top-scoring applicants, occasionally full-tuition for exceptional TOPIK/academic combinations.

Scholarships are applied per-semester and require re-documentation (transcript, sometimes TOPIK retest proof), so keep your academic records organized every term rather than scrambling at renewal time.

## D-2 Visa Sponsorship: Dankook's Specific Process

Dankook's International Office handles D-2 (degree-seeking student) visa sponsorship centrally, and the sequence is fairly standard but with a few Dankook-specific checkpoints:

1. **Admission confirmation** — after acceptance, Dankook issues a Certificate of Admission and Standard Admission Letter required for visa application.
2. **Document submission to the International Office** — passport copy, financial proof (bank statement showing ~$20,000 USD equivalent or sponsor affidavit), and health check results (TB test required for many nationalities) go through Dankook's office, not directly to immigration.
3. **Visa issuance confirmation number** — Dankook requests this from the Korean Immigration Service on your behalf once documents clear; you then apply for the D-2 visa at your home-country Korean embassy/consulate using this number.
4. **Arrival and Alien Registration Card (ARC)** — within 90 days of arrival, register at the Yongin or Cheonan immigration office (campus-dependent) through sessions the International Office often organizes for new cohorts.
5. **Semester renewal** — D-2 visas are tied to enrollment status; dropping below full-time credits or taking an unapproved leave can jeopardize visa validity, so clear any leave-of-absence plans with the International Office first.

Processing from document submission to visa issuance confirmation typically takes 3–4 weeks, so submit materials at least 6–8 weeks before your intended semester start to leave buffer for embassy appointment scheduling.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Dankook good for students without any Korean ability?**
Workable in Business/Engineering English tracks, but most majors and daily campus life assume at least basic Korean — KLI enrollment first is common and advisable.

**Can I live off-campus instead of in the dorm?**
Yes, especially after year one; studios near Jukjeon run ₩400,000–600,000/month plus deposit (jeonse/wolse systems apply), similar or slightly cheaper near Cheonan.

**Does GPA affect visa status?**
Not directly, but falling to part-time or academic probation status can affect enrollment verification needed for visa renewal — keep credits and GPA steady.

## Why Study Korean in Asan Instead of Seoul

Soonchunhyang University Korean Language Institute (순천향대학교 한국어교육원) sits on the university's main campus in Sinchang-myeon, Asan-si, Chungcheongnam-do — about 70 minutes from Seoul by KTX/subway (Cheonan-Asan Station is roughly 15 minutes away by campus shuttle or taxi). Students who choose Asan over Seoul or Busan typically do so for one reason: cost. Monthly living expenses (rent, food, transport) run noticeably lower than in the capital, while the institute still offers a full six-level curriculum, TOPIK-focused electives, and a direct academic bridge into Soonchunhyang University's undergraduate and graduate programs for students who complete Level 4 or higher.

## Program Structure and Weekly Load

The Regular Program runs in four 10-week quarters per year, admitting new students in March, June, September, and December. Classes are split across six proficiency levels (1 through 6), with roughly 20 hours of instruction per week — 4 hours per day, five days a week. Each level ends with a placement/achievement test that determines advancement; students who fail to meet the attendance or grade threshold may be asked to repeat a level.

| Level | Focus | Weekly Hours | Typical TOPIK Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Survival Korean, Hangeul, basic grammar | 20 | TOPIK I (Level 1-2) |
| 3-4 | Intermediate grammar, essay writing | 20 | TOPIK II (Level 3-4) |
| 5-6 | Academic Korean, presentations, debate | 20 | TOPIK II (Level 5-6) |

## Tuition and Fee Breakdown

Fees are billed per quarter and must be paid before the visa/enrollment deadline each term. The table below reflects the most common cost structure; textbook and dormitory fees are billed separately from tuition.

| Item | Amount (KRW) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Application/registration fee | 70,000 | One-time, non-refundable |
| Quarterly tuition | 1,480,000 | Per 10-week term |
| Textbook fee | 60,000 | Per quarter, level-specific set |
| Dormitory (double room) | 450,000 | Per quarter, meals not included |
| Health insurance (NHIS) | ~50,000/month | Mandatory for D-4 visa holders |

Total cost for a first quarter, including registration, tuition, and books, is approximately 1,610,000 KRW — before housing and living expenses.

## Dormitory and Off-Campus Housing in Sinchang-myeon

On-campus dormitory space is limited and allocated first to new international students, particularly those arriving from outside Korea for the first time. Rooms are typically double-occupancy with shared bathrooms and a communal kitchen or cafeteria access. Because Asan is a smaller city than Seoul, off-campus studio apartments (원룸) near campus rent for roughly 350,000-500,000 KRW per month plus utilities, well below equivalent options near major Seoul universities. Students who prefer city amenities often choose housing near Cheonan-Asan Station and commute via the free or low-cost campus shuttle.

## D-4 Visa and Attendance Rules

Accepted students receive a standard certificate of admission used to apply for a D-4 (general training) visa at a Korean embassy or consulate in their home country. Once in Korea, students must maintain at least 90% attendance per quarter — falling below this threshold can jeopardize visa extension eligibility and blocks advancement to the next level regardless of test scores. The institute reports attendance to immigration each term, so chronic absences (including oversleeping or part-time work conflicts) are the single most common reason international students lose visa status here, not academic failure.

## Admission Documents and Application Timeline

Applications are generally due 2-3 months before each quarter's start date to allow time for visa processing. Required documents are broadly consistent across quarters, though the institute may request additional financial proof depending on the applicant's home country.

| Document | Required For |
|---|---|
| Passport copy | All applicants |
| High school or university diploma (apostilled/notarized) | All applicants |
| Bank statement (balance certificate, 10M+ KRW recommended) | Visa sponsorship |
| Passport-size photo | All applicants |
| Application form and study plan | All applicants |

Late applications are sometimes accepted if seats remain, but visa processing time is the real constraint — applicants from countries with longer D-4 review times should apply at the earliest window.

## TOPIK Preparation and Academic Pathway

Because Soonchunhyang University's own degree programs draw a portion of their international enrollment directly from this institute, TOPIK preparation here is oriented toward practical benchmarks: TOPIK 3 is the common minimum for direct pathway consideration into undergraduate programs, while TOPIK 4 or higher opens up scholarship discussions for continuing students. Levels 5-6 include structured TOPIK writing and listening drills alongside academic Korean coursework, rather than treating test prep as a separate track.

## Getting to Campus

Most incoming students fly into Incheon International Airport and either take an airport limousine bus toward Cheonan/Asan or arrange the institute's paid airport pickup service, which is the simpler option for first-time arrivals unfamiliar with Korea's bus systems. From Cheonan-Asan KTX station, the university runs a shuttle bus to the Sinchang-myeon campus; a taxi covers the same distance in about 15-20 minutes.

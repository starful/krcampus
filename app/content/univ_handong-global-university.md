## Why Pohang, Not Seoul, Is Central to the Handong Decision

Handong Global University (한동대학교) sits in Buk-gu, Pohang, a steel-and-shipping city on Korea's southeast coast in Gyeongsangbuk-do (Gyeongbuk), roughly 2.5-3 hours from Seoul by KTX-plus-transfer or intercity bus. This matters more than most rankings pages admit: Handong is not a commuter school with a subway stop outside the gate. The campus is set apart on a hillside, and daily life revolves around the university itself rather than a surrounding downtown. Students weighing Handong against a Seoul-area SKY or Yonsei/Korea/Sogang option should treat the location as a lifestyle choice, not just a line on an admissions form — fewer part-time job options, fewer competing universities nearby for networking, but also lower living costs and a famously close-knit student body.

## Enrolling Without a Department: How the Major System Actually Works

Handong is built around a distinctive "no-department" (무학과) structure. Instead of applying directly into Computer Science or Business, most incoming students enter a broader school or the general track and formally declare their major only after their first year, once they have taken introductory courses across several schools. In practice this means an 18-year-old international applicant does not need to have decided between engineering and law before submitting documents — but it also means academic advising in year one is critical, since major placement can be competitive for popular tracks like the School of ICT Convergence or the Handong International Law School. Applicants should confirm the exact declaration process and any GPA thresholds for their target school directly with the Office of International Affairs, since specific cutoffs shift by cohort.

## Dorm Life Is Not Optional: The Four-Year Residential Community

Where many Korean universities guarantee dorm rooms only to freshmen or out-of-region students, Handong runs its residential system as a core part of its identity, and a large share of students live on campus for all four years rather than moving to off-campus officetels after year one. Dorms are organized into mixed-cohort "family" units with student RAs, shared devotions, and community duties, which is part of why Handong markets itself less on Seoul-style convenience and more on close mentoring and peer accountability. For an international student, this significantly simplifies the housing search that dominates the first month at most Korean universities — but it also means less privacy and a more structured daily schedule than an apartment-based exchange semester elsewhere.

## Chapel, Honor Code, and the Christian Identity Behind the Curriculum

Handong was founded in 1995 as a Christian university, and that identity is not decorative. Chapel attendance is built into the academic schedule for most students, general education includes Christianity-related coursework, and the school is well known across Korea for its honor-code exam culture: many exams are unproctored, with students self-reporting under a no-cheating pledge that dates back to the university's founding. This is frequently cited in Korean media as one of Handong's defining features. Applicants — international or domestic — who are not comfortable with a faith-integrated campus environment should weigh this carefully before applying, since it shapes daily routine (chapel times, dorm devotions) more than at a typical secular Korean university.

## International Admission: Visa, TOPIK, and English-Track Realities

International degree-seeking students typically enter on a D-2 student visa, arranged through Handong's Office of International Affairs after admission, and must complete the standard Korean immigration steps: Alien Registration Card (ARC) within 90 days of arrival, and health-insurance enrollment (NHIS coverage became compulsory for long-term foreign students in 2021). Handong offers a meaningful number of courses in English, particularly within ICT, business, and law tracks, but Korean-medium general education requirements mean TOPIK level 3+ is strongly advisable for students planning to stay past a first-year adjustment period, and some scholarship tiers explicitly reward TOPIK score improvements. Applicants relying entirely on English should verify, program by program, what percentage of required courses are actually offered in English each semester, since this can shift between academic years.

## Pohang Living Costs and Life After Graduation

Because Pohang is smaller and less expensive than Seoul, off-campus rent, food, and transport costs for students who do move out of the dorms tend to run noticeably lower than in Sinchon or Gangnam-adjacent university areas — a meaningful factor for self-funded international students budgeting multi-year stays. The tradeoff is a thinner part-time job market and fewer large employers within walking distance, though Pohang's POSCO-driven industrial economy does create engineering and materials-science internship pipelines that Seoul-based students don't have as easily. Students aiming for Seoul-based corporate jobs after graduation should factor in extra job-hunting travel time in their final year, while those targeting steel, shipbuilding, or heavy-industry R&D roles may find Pohang's local industry ties a direct advantage.

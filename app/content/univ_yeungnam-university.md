## Where Yeungnam University Actually Sits

Yeungnam University (영남대학교) is not in Daegu proper — its main campus is in Gyeongsan-si, a satellite city on the eastern edge of the Daegu metropolitan area in Gyeongsangbuk-do. Subway Line 2 connects Gyeongsan Station to downtown Daegu in roughly 30-40 minutes, so students commonly describe the location as "Daegu-adjacent" rather than "in Daegu." This matters for anyone applying: Gyeongsan is a dedicated university town (it hosts several other campuses), which means cheaper student housing and food than central Daegu, but a genuinely different daily rhythm than a downtown Seoul or Busan campus. For international applicants weighing cost of living, Gyeongsan is one of the more budget-friendly university cities in Korea.

## Campus Scale and Daily Life

Yeungnam is frequently cited among the largest university campuses in Korea by physical land area — figures commonly referenced put the campus at well over 3 million square meters (estimated), which in practice means an internal shuttle bus system is standard for getting between the humanities buildings, the engineering complex, and the dormitories. New students should budget extra transit time between classes compared to a compact urban campus. The scale also supports large sports facilities, a central library, and a botanical/arboretum-style green campus that the university markets as a distinguishing feature versus denser Seoul-area schools.

## Colleges and Where the University's Reputation Concentrates

Yeungnam's academic identity splits into a few clear strengths rather than being uniformly strong across all fields. Engineering and IT Engineering carry historical weight — the university's founding lineage traces partly to technical and textile-engineering colleges, and this heritage still shows in strong chemical engineering, textile engineering, and materials science programs. On the humanities side, the School of Korean Studies and School of Chinese Studies are notable: the university's Institute of Korean Studies (국학연구원) is a long-running research center that gives the Korean Studies track more depth than a typical general-education Korean language program. Business Administration and Pharmacy are also commonly cited as competitive departments for domestic applicants. Medicine and Nursing benefit from the university's own affiliated hospital, Yeungnam University Medical Center, located in Daegu, which gives clinical students placement continuity within the same university system.

## International Admissions Track and TOPIK Expectations

Yeungnam runs a standard international-student admissions track separate from the Korean CSAT-based domestic process, typically evaluating high school transcripts, a self-introduction essay, and either a TOPIK score or an equivalent Korean-proficiency interview for Korean-medium programs. As a rough guide used across similarly ranked regional private universities:

| Program Type | Typical Korean Requirement | Typical English Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Korean-medium undergraduate | TOPIK Level 3-4 (Estimated minimum) | Not usually required |
| Selected English-medium tracks | Not usually required | TOEFL/IELTS or equivalent (varies by department) |
| Graduate research programs | TOPIK Level 3+ recommended | Department-dependent |

Applicants without the target TOPIK level at application time can often enroll conditionally through the university's Korean Language Institute, completing intensive Korean coursework before starting the degree program — a common path at Gyeongbuk-region universities rather than something unique to Yeungnam.

## Visa Path and Settling In Around Gyeongsan

Admitted degree-seeking students receive the standard D-2 student visa (D-4 is for the language-institute-only track, before transitioning to D-2 upon degree enrollment). Because Gyeongsan is a smaller city than Daegu's core districts, immigration matters are typically handled through the Daegu immigration office, and new students should expect to register their Alien Registration Card (ARC) within 90 days of arrival. Off-campus housing near Yeungnam tends to run cheaper than equivalent options near Seoul-area universities, with officetel-style single rooms commonly available; the university dormitories are also a practical first-year option given the campus's size and the distance between the residential area and downtown Gyeongsan.

## Research Identity Beyond the Classroom

What differentiates Yeungnam from other regional comprehensive universities is less about overall national ranking and more about specific concentrations: the Korean Studies institute's long publication history, the engineering college's textile and chemical-materials lineage, and a hospital-linked medical track. Prospective applicants choosing between Yeungnam and other Daegu-Gyeongbuk universities should weigh these specific strengths against their intended major rather than comparing on general prestige alone, since the university's reputation is genuinely uneven across departments in a way that matters more for career outcomes than the school's overall size or campus scale.

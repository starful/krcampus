## 1. School Overview

The University of Ulsan (UOU) Korean Language Center offers intensive language training for international students. Located in Korea’s industrial powerhouse, the center provides a focused academic environment with a capacity of 300 students. This ensures personalized attention and strong administrative support.

* **Address:** 93 Daehak-ro, Nam-gu, Ulsan, 44610, South Korea
* **Key Features:** Affordable tuition, on-campus dorm access, systematic TOPIK prep, cultural excursions (including Hyundai industry tours), and pathways to UOU degree programs.

The center is ideal for students seeking quality education in a safe, cost-efficient metropolitan city without the high living costs of Seoul.

---

## 2. Programs & Schedule

The **Regular Program** runs four terms per year (Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter) and covers levels 1 (beginner) to 6 (advanced).

* **Hours:** 20 hours per week (Monday to Friday, 09:00–13:00); 200 hours over 10 weeks per term.
* **Class Size:** Average of 12 to 15 students.
* **Curriculum:**
  * **Levels 1–2:** Pronunciation, basic grammar, survival dialogue.
  * **Levels 3–4:** Idiomatic expressions, social discussions, reading simple news.
  * **Levels 5–6:** Professional communication, academic writing, cultural analysis.

## 4. Admissions & D-4 Visa Steps

To study for more than 90 days, you must obtain a D-4 (General Student) visa:

1. **Prepare Documents:** Application form with photo, passport copy, Apostilled or Consular-verified graduation certificate/transcripts, and bank certificate (showing USD 10,000+).
2. **Submit Application:** Send digital copies via email, followed by physical originals via express mail.
3. **Document Review:** UOU reviews academic background and financial status.
4. **Pay Tuition:** Wire two terms of tuition (2,400,000 KRW) plus the 100,000 KRW application fee.
5. **Receive COA:** UOU sends the physical Certificate of Admission (COA) after payment verification.
6. **Apply for D-4 Visa:** Submit the COA, passport, and financial proof to the local Korean Embassy or Consulate.

## 5. TOPIK & University Pathway

UOU provides structured preparation and pathways for students pursuing higher education in Korea:

* **TOPIK Prep:** Special preparation classes and practice tests ahead of official exams.
* **Admission Support:** Dedicated academic counseling for entry into UOU’s undergraduate and graduate departments.
* **Entry Requirements:** TOPIK Level 3 for general majors, Level 4 for business and humanities.
* **Scholarships:** Language center students who complete at least two terms and achieve TOPIK Level 3 or 4 can receive a 30% to 50% tuition waiver on their first undergraduate semester.

---

## 6. Dormitory & Living in the City

* **On-Campus Dormitory:** Double-occupancy rooms equipped with desks, wardrobes, and internet. Facilities include laundry rooms, study lounges, and gyms. Costs range from 800,000 to 1,200,000 KRW per 4-month semester (meals excluded).
* **Cultural Experience Programs:** Activities include pottery making, Taekwondo, city tours of Taehwagang National Garden, and visits to nearby industrial sites.
* **Living in Ulsan:** A safe coastal city with living costs 30-40% lower than Seoul. The Nam-gu campus district ("Daehak-ro") offers budget-friendly dining, cafes, and easy public transit to downtown and the KTX station.

## 7. FAQ

### Can I apply if I only graduated from high school?
Yes. The minimum requirement is a high school diploma. You must submit your graduation certificate with an Apostille or Korean Consular seal.

### Can I work part-time while studying on a D-4 visa?
Yes, but only after completing six months of study and maintaining an attendance rate of 90% or higher. You must secure permission from immigration and the university first.

### Is the dormitory guaranteed for language students?
Priority is given to incoming international students. If you apply within the specified period and pay the fees on time, you are highly likely to secure a room.

### How do I pay the tuition fees from abroad?
UOU will issue an invoice with a dedicated virtual bank account. You must wire the exact amount in KRW or USD via international bank transfer (such as SWIFT or Flywire).

### What is the refund policy if my D-4 visa is denied?
Submit a refund application with the official visa rejection letter. UOU will refund the tuition fee, but the 100,000 KRW application fee is non-refundable.
